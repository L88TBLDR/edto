    Hub

    EDTO = a two-engine operation where the one-engine-inoperative diversion time to an enroute alternate exceeds the CAAS threshold of 60 min. The A320 is approved to a maximum diversion time of 120 min. Everything else on this page answers one of four questions: where is the segment, which aerodrome, how much fuel, what changes the decision.
